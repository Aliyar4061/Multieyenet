A Backbone-Diverse Ensemble for
Dual-Modality Ocular Screening

Unveiling asymmetric robustness and per-class clinical risks in deep learning for anterior-segment and fundus disease classification.

Abstract

Objectives: To develop and validate a unified deep learning framework for automated multi-class screening across two anatomically distinct ophthalmic imaging modalities and to evaluate its structural robustness under simulated real-world imaging variability.


Methods: Seven backbone architectures (convolutional and transformer-based) were evaluated. Top-performing configurations were combined into modality-specific, backbone-diverse ensembles optimized via Sequential Least SQuares Programming (SLSQP). Performance was assessed via stratified cross-validation, architectural ablation, and simulated domain-shift perturbations.


Results: The ensemble achieved 98.85% accuracy (AUC 0.9990) on the anterior dataset and 96.71% (AUC 0.9977) on the fundus dataset. Ablation revealed that removing SE/GCN modules significantly improved baseline performance in specific contexts. Robustness testing revealed a striking modality-dependent asymmetry, and granular analysis exposed elevated false-negative rates in high-risk conditions: Uveitis (9.22%) and Glaucoma (5.26%).

Key Innovations

⚖️ Dual-Modality Benchmarking

Unified evaluation pipeline across 5-class anterior and 4-class fundus datasets under a consistent experimental protocol.

🧠 SLSQP-Optimized Ensemble

Mathematically constrained weight fusion of top-3 backbones to decorrelate architecture-specific errors and maximize reliability.

🔬 Controlled Architectural Ablation

Empirical evidence challenging the universal benefit of SE and GCN modules, preventing unnecessary optimization overhead.

🛡️ Modality-Dependent Robustness

Systematic stress-testing under 4 domain shifts (brightness, contrast, Gaussian noise, resolution reduction).

⚠️ Per-Class Safety Analysis

Explicit reporting of False Negative Rates (FNR) and bootstrapped 95% Confidence Intervals for clinical transparency.

👁️ XAI Integration

Grad-CAM visualizations for both CNN and ViT backbones to ensure anatomical plausibility and build clinical trust.
